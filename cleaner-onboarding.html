Our standards

These apply on every visit, in every home.

  • Arrive on time, inside your arrival window. If you will be late, tell DRC before the window starts.
  • Check in and out on the work order when you arrive and leave.
  • Follow the work order. Client notes come first: products they want used, rooms to skip, pets, alarm and lock instructions.
  • Take before and after photos of every room and every add on, and upload them to the work order.
  • Finish the full checklist. If you cannot finish something, tell DRC before you leave.
  • Respect privacy. Do not open private drawers, mail or documents unless the job requires it. Never photograph people or personal papers, and never post a client’s home online.
  • Come alone. No guests, children or pets of your own. No smoking or vaping on the property.
  • Keep your phone away except for the work order and DRC.
  • Talk to DRC, not around it. Do not share your number with clients, discuss pay, or accept work from a DRC client directly.
  • Leave it secure. Doors locked, alarm set if asked, lights as you found them, keys or codes kept private.
  • Report what you notice. Leaks, broken fixtures or anything needing repair goes on the work order so our maintenance team can help the client.

Chemical safety

Some common cleaners create toxic gas when they mix. This is the most important part of the guide.

Never mix cleaning products. Use one product at a time, rinse the surface with water before switching, and keep the room ventilated.
Never mixBleach with ammonia, including some glass cleaners
Never mixBleach with vinegar or acid based toilet and tile cleaners
Never mixBleach with rubbing alcohol
Never mixHydrogen peroxide with vinegar in the same bottle
Never mixTwo different drain cleaners
Never mixOven cleaner with any other product

If something mixes by accident

  • Leave the room right away and get fresh air. Open windows only if you can do it safely on the way out.
  • Call Poison Control at 1 800 222 1222, or 911 if anyone has trouble breathing.
  • Tell DRC as soon as you are safe. Do not go back in until the air clears.

Everyday safety

  • Read the label before using any product you have not used before.
  • Wear gloves, and keep products away from children, pets and food.
  • Close every bottle and take your products with you when you leave.

Laundry, appliances and yard work

Some clients book more than cleaning. Only do what the work order lists.

Laundry and linens

  • Check pockets and read care labels. Separate lights, darks and delicates.
  • Use cold water unless the label or client says otherwise.
  • Use bleach, fabric softener or scented products only if the client approved them.
  • Air dry anything labeled delicate or hang dry.
  • Fold neatly and put items away where the client asked. If you do not know, fold and leave them on the bed.
  • Fresh linens: strip, wash, and make the bed neatly with corners tucked and pillows arranged.

Inside appliances

  • Fridge: remove items, wipe shelves and drawers with mild soap and water, dry, and return items in order. Never throw anything away unless the work order says to.
  • Oven: check if it is self cleaning. Follow the work order and the product label, ventilate, and wipe away all residue.
  • Microwave and dishwasher: wipe inside, door seals and controls.

Landscaping and yard

  • Do only the tasks on the work order: mowing, edging, trimming, blowing walkways or bagging debris.
  • Never spray weed killer, fertilizer or pest control unless the work order specifically says so.
  • Watch for sprinkler heads, pets and children. Close every gate behind you.

Your responsibilities

We are serious about our clients. These terms are part of the agreement you sign below.

  • Missed or incomplete work. If a client reports something missed or not done to our standard, you go back and complete it at no extra pay, at a time DRC schedules.
  • Damage. If anything is damaged, stop, take photos and report it to DRC before you leave. You are responsible for damage caused by carelessness or by not following this guide, including the cost of repair or replacement. DRC may hold payment for that job until it is resolved.
  • Correct products. You bring the right products for each surface, or use the client’s products when the work order says to.
  • Background check. You agree to a background check before your first job.
