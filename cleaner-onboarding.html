Our standards

These apply on every job. The ones for your type of work are listed too.

  • Arrive on time, inside your arrival window. If you will be late, tell DRC before the window starts.
  • Check in and out on the work order when you arrive and leave.
  • Follow the work order. Client notes come first: pets, gate, alarm and lock instructions, and anything to skip.
  • Take before and after photos of your work and upload them to the work order.
  • Respect privacy. Never photograph people or personal papers, and never post a client’s home online.
  • Bring only your approved crew. No guests, children or pets of your own. No smoking or vaping on the property.
  • Talk to DRC, not around it. Do not share your number with clients, discuss pay, or accept work from a DRC client directly.
  • Leave it secure. Gates closed, doors locked, alarm set if asked, keys or codes kept private.
  • Report what you notice. Leaks, broken fixtures or anything needing repair goes on the work order so our maintenance team can help the client.
  • Cleaning: finish the full checklist, and tell DRC before you leave if anything could not be done. Do not open private drawers, mail or documents unless the job requires it. Keep your phone away except for the work order.
  • Cleaning: use the client’s own products when the work order says so.
  • Organizing: nothing leaves the home without the client’s approval, and personal papers and medicine are set aside, never read.
  • Lighting: check the weather before you go. No ladder or roof work in rain, ice or high wind, and never work a two story roof without a second person on site.
  • Lighting: work outside only unless the work order says otherwise. Protect shrubs, gutters and the roofline, and leave every outlet cover closed.
  • Carpet: move furniture only with the client’s OK, protect walls and door frames from hoses, and never leave carpets soaking wet.
  • Washing: test a hidden spot first, cover plants, outlets and lights, and never point a high pressure tip at windows, siding seams, wood or people.
  • Handyman: never enter a home unless an adult is there, confirm the scope before you start, and get DRC’s OK before any extra work.
  • Background checks. You need one before your first job, and we prefer every person on your crew to have one too.

Home organizing

Closets, pantries, garages, kitchens and kids rooms. Organizing jobs are quoted per project, and DRC tells you the pay before you accept.

  • Take before photos of every space first, then after photos of the finished space and labels.
  • Nothing leaves the home without the client saying so. Set donate, trash and keep piles and have the client approve them.
  • Group by category, put everyday items within reach, and label containers clearly.
  • Only buy products the client approved. Keep receipts and upload them to the work order.
  • Respect privacy. Papers, mail, medicine and personal items are set aside for the client, never read or photographed.
  • Leave it clean. Wipe shelves before restocking, and haul off only the approved trash.

Christmas and holiday lighting

Installs, takedowns and storage for homes. DRC sets the pay per job from the design and the home, and tells you before you accept.

Safety first

  • Ladders: three points of contact, set on firm level ground, a spotter on every two story job, and never work in rain, ice or high wind.
  • Roofs: no roof work on wet or frosted shingles, and use fall protection on steep pitches.
  • Power: outdoor rated lights, cords and timers only, plugged into GFCI outlets. Never overload a circuit or run cords through windows or doors that pinch them.
  • No damage: use clips only. Never nail, staple or screw into shingles, gutters, siding or trim.

Every job

  • Before photos of the home, then a night photo of the lit home uploaded to the work order.
  • Test every strand before it goes up, and leave the timer set the way the client asked.
  • Takedowns in January: remove every clip, coil and label the lights, and leave them where the client asked.
  • Leave the yard clean: no clips, packaging or zip ties left behind.

Carpet and upholstery cleaning

Carpet jobs are quoted one by one. DRC tells you the pay before you accept, and you can say no.

Professional equipment only. Carpet cleaning companies must use a truck mounted system or a commercial grade portable extractor. Rental machines from a hardware store are not accepted.
  • Inspect first. Photograph stains, wear, pet damage and loose seams before you start, and note anything that may not come out.
  • Pretreat and test. Check fiber type and colorfastness on a hidden spot before using any product.
  • Protect the home. Corner guards on walls and door frames, hoses kept off finished floors, and the door closed on hot days.
  • Dry fast. Extract thoroughly, set fans if needed, and put tabs or blocks under furniture legs you move back.
  • Tell the client how long to stay off the carpet, and upload before and after photos to the work order.

Chemical safety

Some common cleaners create toxic gas when they mix. This is the most important part of the guide.

Never mix cleaning products. Use one product at a time, rinse the surface with water before switching, and keep the room ventilated.
Never mixBleach with ammonia, including some glass cleaners
Never mixBleach with vinegar or acid based toilet and tile cleaners
Never mixBleach with rubbing alcohol
Never mixHydrogen peroxide with vinegar in the same bottle
Never mixTwo different drain cleaners
Never mixOven cleaner with any other product

If something mixes by accident

  • Leave the room right away and get fresh air. Open windows only if you can do it safely on the way out.
  • Call Poison Control at 1 800 222 1222, or 911 if anyone has trouble breathing.
  • Tell DRC as soon as you are safe. Do not go back in until the air clears.

Everyday safety

  • Read the label before using any product you have not used before.
  • Wear gloves, and keep products away from children, pets and food.
  • Close every bottle and take your products with you when you leave.

This guide is general safety information, not professional or medical advice, and it does not replace product labels or Safety Data Sheets. As an independent contractor you are responsible for reading labels, using products as directed and working safely. Laundry, appliances and yard work

Some clients book more than cleaning. Only do what the work order lists.

Laundry and linens

  • Check pockets and read care labels. Separate lights, darks and delicates.
  • Use cold water unless the label or client says otherwise.
  • Use bleach, fabric softener or scented products only if the client approved them.
  • Air dry anything labeled delicate or hang dry.
  • Fold neatly and put items away where the client asked. If you do not know, fold and leave them on the bed.
  • Fresh linens: strip, wash, and make the bed neatly with corners tucked and pillows arranged.

Inside appliances

  • Fridge: remove items, wipe shelves and drawers with mild soap and water, dry, and return items in order. Never throw anything away unless the work order says to.
  • Oven: check if it is self cleaning. Follow the work order and the product label, ventilate, and wipe away all residue.
  • Microwave and dishwasher: wipe inside, door seals and controls.

Landscaping and yard

  • Do only the tasks on the work order: mowing, edging, trimming, blowing walkways or bagging debris.
  • Never spray weed killer, fertilizer or pest control unless the work order specifically says so.
  • Watch for sprinkler heads, pets and children. Close every gate behind you.
