      Questions

      Good to know

      Do I need to provide cleaning supplies?

      No. We can bring ours, or you can leave out your own and we'll use only those. If you have marble, granite or other natural stone, tell us and we'll use a stone safe, pH neutral cleaner.

      How much does a cleaning cost?

      Most 3 bed, 2 bath homes run $190 to $245 for Essential Home Cleaning and $335 to $425 for Signature Deep Cleaning. Your estimate uses your home's size and condition, and we confirm the final price with you before booking.

      What happens after I send my estimate?

      Someone from DRC calls you to confirm your price, answer questions and set a date. Once you approve, we email a secure Square payment link with your price and terms. Your date is locked in when payment comes through. Payments are non refundable, and if we miss anything we come back and make it right.

      Where do you clean?

      We currently serve the Dallas, Denver, Nashville and Phoenix areas. Somewhere else? Send your estimate anyway and we'll let you know.

      Do I need to be home?

      No. Many clients give us a lockbox or door code. Either way, you get before and after photos of every room when we're done.
