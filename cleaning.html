    Questions

    Good to know

    Do I need to provide cleaning supplies?

    No. You can choose to have us bring our products, or leave out your own and we'll use only those. If you have marble, granite or other natural stone, tell us and we'll use a stone-safe, pH-neutral cleaner.

    What's the difference between organic and no-microplastics?

    Organic and unscented uses vinegar-based and fragrance-free products, which is great for allergies, kids and pets. No microplastics goes a step further: plastic-free tools and products, and no microfiber cloths, which shed tiny plastic fibers.

    Are your cleaners background-checked and insured?

    Yes. Every cleaner passes a background check and carries their own liability and auto insurance before they're sent to a home.

    How do I pay?

    After you approve your confirmed price, we send a secure Square card link or Zelle details. Your date is booked once payment is received.

    Do I need to be home?

    No. Many clients give us a lockbox or door code. Either way, you get before and after photos of every room when we're done.

    Do you offer recurring cleaning?

    Yes. Weekly, every two weeks and monthly clients get a lower price on every visit.

    Can you fix things while you're there?

    Yes. We're part of DRC Maintenance, so we can also handle repairs, paint, junk removal and lawn care. Just mention it in your notes.
