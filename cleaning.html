      Questions

      Good to know

      Do I need to provide cleaning supplies?

      No. We can bring ours, or you can leave out your own and we'll use only those. If you have marble, granite or other natural stone, tell us and we'll use a stone-safe, pH-neutral cleaner.

      What's the difference between organic and no-microplastics?

      Organic and unscented means vinegar-based, fragrance-free products, which is great for allergies, kids and pets. No microplastics goes a step further: plastic-free tools and products, and no microfiber cloths, which shed tiny plastic fibers.

      Are your cleaners background-checked?

      Yes. Every cleaner passes a background check before they're sent to a home.

      What happens after I send my estimate?

      Someone from DRC calls you to confirm your price, answer questions and set a date. Once you approve, we send a secure card link or Zelle details, and your date is locked in when payment comes through.

      Where do you clean?

      We currently serve the Dallas, Denver, Nashville and Phoenix areas. Somewhere else? Send your estimate anyway and we'll let you know.

      Do I need to be home?

      No. Many clients give us a lockbox or door code. Either way, you get before and after photos of every room when we're done.

      Do you offer recurring cleaning?

      Yes. Weekly, every two weeks and monthly clients get a lower price on every visit.

      Can you fix things while you're there?

      Yes. We're part of DRC Maintenance, so we can also handle repairs, paint, junk removal and lawn care. Add it to your estimate or send a request in the repairs section, and we'll quote it from photos first.

      Ready for a cleaner home?

      Two minutes for your price, then we'll give you a call.
