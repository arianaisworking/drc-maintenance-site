Good to know

Is my price final?

Essential Home Cleaning prices are instant estimates based on your home's size. Signature Deep Cleaning shows a starting range, and we confirm your final quote after a quick review. Either way, nothing is charged until you approve it.

Will it be the same cleaner each time?

Yes. Recurring clients keep the same cleaner, with your preferences saved for every visit.

Do I need to be home?

No. Many clients share a lockbox or door code. You get before and after photos when we finish.

Do you bring supplies?

We can bring ours, or use yours. Organic and microplastic free options are available.
