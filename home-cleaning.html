Questions

Is my price final?

Essential Home Cleaning prices are estimates based on your home's size. Signature Deep Cleaning shows a starting range, and we confirm your final quote after a quick review. Nothing is charged until you approve it.

Will it be the same cleaner each time?

Yes. Recurring clients keep the same cleaner, with your preferences saved for every visit.

Can you handle laundry, organizing and repairs too?

Yes. Add laundry and extras when you see your price, and tell us about organizing, carpet or repairs. We quote those for you separately.

Do I need to be home?

No. Many clients share a lockbox or door code. You receive photos of every room when we finish.

Do you bring supplies?

We bring ours or use yours. Organic and microplastic free options are available.
