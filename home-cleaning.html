Questions

Is my price final?

Home cleaning prices are estimates based on your home's size. Deep cleaning shows a starting range, and we confirm your final quote after a quick review. Laundry, organizing, repairs and yard care are quoted for you. Nothing is charged until you approve it.

Who will be in my home?

A background checked DRC professional. Your preferences are saved, so every visit is held to the same standard.

Can you handle more than cleaning?

Yes. Choose everything you need when you start, or tell us in your own words. We take care of laundry, dishes, organizing, repairs, landscaping and move in or move out.

Do I need to be home?

No. Many clients share a lockbox or door code. You receive photos of every room when we finish.

Do you bring supplies?

We bring ours or use yours. Organic and microplastic free options are available.
